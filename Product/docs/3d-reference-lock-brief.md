# 3D Reference Lock — Brief

Milestone: M-REFERENCE-LOCKED-APPLE-MONO-UI-AND-BOARD-HITTEST-FIX-1
Date: 2026-06-11

## Lock statement

1. **The final 3D style is NOT approved.** No further 3D visual direction may be guessed
   by the agent. Final battlefield design decisions wait for user-provided reference
   images/videos and their documented analysis.
2. **The current 3D battlefield is a technical prototype only** — a procedural low-poly
   placeholder proving the architecture (scene, board mapping, animation, fallbacks,
   licensing pipeline). It must never be described as the intended final art.
3. **Future battlefield design must be based on user references.** The user has begun
   providing reference imagery (Mahabharata-era unit sets: hero archer, horse archers,
   camel archers, war-elephant archers, minister's and king's war chariots, armored
   elephant variants). Analysis lives in `3d-kurukshetra-reference-expectation.md` and in
   `design-references/README.md` guidance; raw images are stored in the repo only if the
   user confirms they may be.
4. **"Similar to references" means** matching the visual direction: atmosphere, camera
   feel, lighting, terrain identity, unit silhouettes and material quality, movement
   style, capture-effect style, fidelity tier — **never copying copyrighted assets,
   models, characters, or trade dress.**
5. **No copyrighted or unlicensed models may be used.** Every future asset must pass the
   manifest policy in `assets/3d/README.md` (source, author, license, AI-generation
   disclosure) before it ships.
6. **No final 3D realism claim** may appear in UI copy, README, docs, or tags until
   approved models/assets/animations exist in the repo and screenshots prove the bar.

## What may continue meanwhile

- Bug fixes, performance, fallbacks, and board-interaction correctness of the prototype.
- Reference ANALYSIS work (text), asset-brief drafting, and licensing research.

## What is paused

- Any new procedural art direction, materials, silhouettes, or effects styling beyond
  bug-level fixes — these would be guesses the user has explicitly rejected.
